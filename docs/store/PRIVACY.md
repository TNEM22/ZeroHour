# Zero Hour — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

This policy covers the **Zero Hour — New Tab Countdown** browser extension and
the **Zero Hour** browser themes.

## The short version

Zero Hour does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data. It makes
no network requests, has no analytics and no accounts, and none of your
information reaches the developer or anyone else.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves your own settings: the countdown date, title, tagline, the
message shown when the countdown ends, your chosen colour palette, and any
shortcut tiles you add yourself.

These settings are saved with the browser's built-in storage
(`chrome.storage.sync`). If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned
on, your browser syncs them across your own devices through your browser
account. The developer has no access to them.

## Permissions, and what each one does with your data

- **storage**: saves the settings listed above, in your browser.
- **search**: sends what you type in the new tab search box to your own default
  search engine, just like the browser's address bar. The extension does not
  see, log or keep your searches.
- **favicon**: shows site icons on shortcut tiles, read from the browser's local
  icon cache.
- **topSites** (optional, only if you turn it on): reads your most-visited sites
  so they can appear as shortcut tiles. This list is read on your device when
  the page is shown and is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **scripting and host access** (optional, only if you turn it on): applies a
  static stylesheet so scrollbars and text selection on web pages match your
  palette. No scripts run on web pages, and no page content is read or sent.

## What is never collected

No personally identifiable information, browsing history, page contents,
location, financial or health information, authentication details, or
communications. No data is sold or shared with third parties, or used for
advertising, credit scoring or any purpose unrelated to the extension.

## The themes

The Zero Hour browser themes contain no code at all. They only set the browser's
colours and images, and cannot collect or transmit anything.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings from your browser.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Any change that
involves collecting data would also require a new store review.

## Contact

For questions, use the developer contact email shown on the Chrome Web Store
listing.
